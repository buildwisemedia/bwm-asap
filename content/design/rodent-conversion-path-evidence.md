# Rodent conversion-path evidence

Path: broad concern → answer-first scope → correct specific-service owner → inspection pattern → service components → attributable proof → FAQ → call or inspection request.

- Phone and form choices stay available without forcing a single channel.
- Every intent card routes to a distinct existing owner URL.
- The form carries `lead_id`, `source_page`, `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `gclid`, and `fbclid`.
- Local review intercepts submission before network delivery and exposes an explicit no-send result.
- Live delivery, response time, booking, revenue, and customer acceptance remain untested and unclaimed.
